The tide always brings someone home.

Tidewater Cove rescues stranded seals and sea otters along a stretch of the Oregon coast, nurses them back to the water, and runs the public tide-pool center where most visitors first learn their names.

Three things happen at the Cove

Rescue

A trained response team answers the stranding hotline year-round, reaching any beach on our 40-mile stretch of coastline within two hours of a call.

Rehabilitate

Animals recover in nursery pools built to mimic tidal conditions, under the care of two staff veterinarians and a rotating crew of trained volunteers.

Educate

Free low-tide walks run most weekends, pairing a marine biologist with visitors of any age at the exposed tide pools below the center.
