Surveys for every stage of a property

Residential, commercial-industrial, agricultural, and public work. Pick a survey type to see what it covers.

Boundary survey

We locate or set the corner monuments of your parcel and show the lines on a certified drawing, so fences, driveways, and additions go exactly where they belong.

  • Typical for buying land, fence disputes, lake lots
  • You receive a signed survey drawing and set monuments
Request a boundary survey

ALTA/NSPS land title survey

A specialized boundary survey built for title insurance. It discloses every survey-related title issue so lenders, title attorneys, and buyers can make informed decisions about a piece of real estate.

  • Typical for commercial purchases and refinancing
  • Shows boundaries, improvements, easements, encroachments
Request an ALTA survey

Building permit survey

Shows the parcel boundary, topographic conditions, and existing and proposed improvements, so your structure gets built without encroachment, elevation, or density problems.

  • Typical for new homes, garages, lake cabins
  • Shows setbacks, elevations, proposed footprint
Request a permit survey

Subdivision survey

Minor subdivisions of one or two lots often need only a boundary survey and a legal description of each portion, which an abstract company or attorney then uses to prepare and file the deeds. Larger plats are handled start to finish.

  • Typical for splitting family land, selling a lot
  • You receive new legal descriptions and monuments
Request a subdivision survey

Construction staking

We put the design on the ground: staking residential homes, additions, and site improvements so your contractor builds to the approved plan and the right elevation.

  • Typical for new home foundations and site work
  • You receive offset stakes and elevation marks
Request staking

Topographic survey

We map ground elevations, contours, and existing features so engineers and builders can design grading, drainage, and shoreland improvements with real numbers.

  • Typical for site design, drainage, shoreland lots
  • Shows contours, spot elevations, features
Request a topographic survey

Agricultural & rural surveys

Farmland and rural parcels described by section, township, and range. We retrace original government survey lines and set corners for sales, splits, and estate settlements.

  • Typical for farm sales, estate splits, rural building sites
  • Works from PLSS section and quarter-section corners
Request a rural survey
